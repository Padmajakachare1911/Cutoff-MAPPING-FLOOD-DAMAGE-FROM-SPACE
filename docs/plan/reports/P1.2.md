# P1.2 — Speckle filter (improved Lee sigma)

## What was built
`src/cutoff/geo/speckle.py`: `lee_sigma(img, window=7, target=3, sigma=0.9, looks=4.4, point_pct=98.0)`, an
implementation of the full improved Lee sigma filter (Lee et al. 2009, IEEE TGRS 47(1)), within the 2 h time box.
The refined-Lee fallback was not needed.

1. **Point targets:** a pixel whose 3×3 target window holds ≥ `POINT_K` = 5 pixels above the image's 98th
   percentile is kept unfiltered (K = 5 as in the paper).
2. **Sigma range:** `sigma_range(looks, sigma)` solves numerically, for Gamma(L, 1/L) intensity speckle, for the
   interval [a1, a2] that holds 0.9 of the mass and has a truncated mean of exactly 1 (no bias). It also returns the
   truncated CV η_v. For L = 4.4: a1 = 0.398, a2 = 2.024, η_v = 0.380. The a-priori mean is the 3×3 MMSE (Lee)
   estimate; the range is [a1·x0, a2·x0].
3. **MMSE:** the estimate uses the in-range pixels of the 7×7 window, with η_v. If no pixel is in range, the 3×3
   prior is used.

NaN-safe: NaN pixels are left out of every neighbourhood, NaN stays NaN, and no new NaN appears. Linear in, float32
linear out.

## Tests
```
python -m pytest tests/test_p1_speckle.py -q -p no:warnings   -> 6 passed
```
- Flat gamma field (L = 4.4): CV 0.476 → 0.115, i.e. 24 % of the input (requirement < 40 %); mean ratio 0.997.
- Step edge 0.02 | 0.20: each side's mean is preserved within 5 %, and the columns next to the edge stay on their side.
- A 3×3 bright point target: its centre is returned unchanged.
- NaNs stay NaN and don't spread; pixels outside the 7×7 reach of a hole are unchanged.
- Extra checks: the sigma range has mass 0.9 and truncated mean 1; all-NaN and all-zero inputs.

Runtime: 3.5 s for a 2100 × 1400 band (the trishuli_north grid size) on this laptop (numpy, 49 shifted views).

## Deviations
None. Note: the "isolated bright point" test uses a 3×3 target, because the plan's rule (≥ k pixels above Z98 in the
3×3 window, k = 5) by design does not protect a single bright pixel.
