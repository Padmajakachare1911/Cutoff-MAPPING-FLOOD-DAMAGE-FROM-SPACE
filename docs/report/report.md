# Cutoff / Flood exposure and access

<p class="notice">Educational prototype - not an operational tool. Exposure is not confirmed damage.</p><h2>Abstract</h2><p>Cutoff is an educational prototype for rescue planning. Given an area and event time, it combines same-track radar change, a flood model, terrain and pre-event infrastructure to answer three questions: where did the event leave evidence, what was exposed, and who may be cut off? The product is a map dashboard and one-page situation report driven by a validated Results record.</p><h2>System overview</h2><img src="C:/yaseer code/idk/multimodal/flood-damage/docs/report/pipeline.png" width="520"><h2>Current delivery</h2><p>P1-P3 algorithms and the P4 product are implemented. Pipeline/CLI, strict PDF, dashboard, feature CI, isolated-install checks, guarded evaluation and runtime tooling are delivered. Real hazard case studies completed; full access integration is awaiting data remediation. E4/freeze and human rehearsal remain explicit handoff checks.</p><h2>Users and boundaries</h2><p>Settlement-scale prioritisation and evidence review, rather than structural damage assessment. Every display carries the prototype disclaimer. Missing observations and unmapped access are represented as uncertainty.</p><p class="source">Source: docs/plan/00_MASTER_PLAN.md; docs/plan/reports/P4.1-P4.7.md</p>

# Methods / consistent observations

<h2>Acquisition and preprocessing</h2><p>Choose a post-event pass strictly after the supplied UTC timestamp and up to three pre-event passes from the same relative orbit/direction. Require VV/VH and adequate footprint coverage. Bare dates mean end of day. Store linear sigma-zero, apply Lee Sigma and average pre-event scenes in linear units. Fast processing uses CDSE Sentinel Hub; full processing uses SNAP. Buffered Copernicus GLO-30 provides slope, HAND and drainage.</p><h2>Model and evidence</h2><p>The seven-channel U-Net uses pre/post VV/VH dB, their differences and slope, with the released no-slope model holding the slope channel at its training mean. Calibration/threshold selection uses validation events only. A scene-wide robust change scale identifies coherent decreases/increases in the drainage corridor. Terrain/S2 evidence adjusts confidence; shadow/layover and invalid data become no observation.</p><h2>Exposure and access</h2><p>Intersect hazard with strictly pre-event OSM. Split roads at valid intersections, retain keyed parallel edges, and compare baseline/post-closure travel time to mapped targets. Scenario A uses proximity to all hazard, B sustained high-confidence overlap, C bridge approaches. Report connected/degraded/cut off/unknown separately and retain trail access. No operational closure is asserted.</p><h2>Product integrity</h2><p>Validate stage outputs on the shared UTM grid before consumers run. Optional optical errors continue; infrastructure errors publish a partial report; core acquisition errors preserve their diagnostic. The dashboard and PDF read Results statistics. OSM caches are isolated by snapshot/settings in P4.</p><p class="source">Source: docs/plan/05_contracts.md; configs/default.yaml; docs/plan/reports/P1_HANDOFF.md; P2_HANDOFF.md; P3_HANDOFF.md</p>

# Limitations / evidence still pending

<h2>E4 and E6</h2><p>No real EMSR927 comparison has occurred. The check-only harness is complete and tested with synthetic polygons. After corrected-data integration, freeze clean code/config/model and run it once. The optical observed-event layer represents mass movement, creating a definition mismatch with SAR water. Point-only reference buildings do not establish footprint recall. Human tile taxonomy remains necessary.</p><p>E6 tooling and algorithm tests exist; real buffered access/scenario/dropout measurements require a verified OSM v2 key or dated caches. The upstream v1 geometry fallback is restricted. The teammate owns the underlying data-integrity remediation.</p><h2>Scientific and operational limits</h2><p>Overpasses can miss the peak; this is not early warning. Ten-metre pixels omit narrow roads/bridges. Shadow/layover are unobservable. Wet mud may resemble water; rough debris is statistical change. Surface DEM and gorge smoothing influence HAND/slope. Permanent-water scores can include steep back-slopes and are excluded from hazard exposure. OSM incompleteness can change access status; unknown means unmapped, not safe.</p><h2>Readiness limits</h2><p>Competition permission for cached/fast processing and pretrained encoders is unconfirmed. Independent teammate fresh-clone and unprepared-AOI rehearsal remain pending. Submission evidence must retain these distinctions; targets and synthetic demonstrations are not real-world accuracy results.</p><p class="source">Source: docs/results/E4.md; docs/results/E6.md; docs/results/E7.md; docs/plan/reports/organiser_answers.md; issues #25 and #31</p>
