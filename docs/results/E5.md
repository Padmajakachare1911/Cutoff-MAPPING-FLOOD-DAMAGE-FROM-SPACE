## Hypothesis (pre-registered)
**H3:** a cross-track pre/post pair gives a higher false-positive rate on stable steep terrain than the same-track
pair: `stable_steep_fpr(cross) > stable_steep_fpr(same)`. The reason is that the viewing geometry changes between
ascending and descending passes, so terrain-induced backscatter changes appear as "change" on steep slopes.
